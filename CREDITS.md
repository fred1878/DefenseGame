## Town art (isometric 2:1 packs from itch.io)

Only the sprites the town draws ship, packed into the atlases in `public/assets/town/iso/` (with baked colour grading; the Kyndrael sprites have their baked cast shadows removed). The packs themselves are not redistributed, and **none of these files may be used to train AI models or redistributed as a standalone asset pack** (raw or edited). The packer is `scripts/build-town-iso-assets.py`; the fog, vignette, light and smoke textures are generated by that script.

- **FREE Isometric Terrain & Nature Tiles by Antahonist** ([antahonist.itch.io](https://antahonist.itch.io/free-isometric-terrain-nature-tiles)): ground tiles and corner autotiles (`town-iso-ground`). Free for commercial games, modification allowed; no resale or redistribution on their own or in another pack; no AI training. "Terrain & Nature by Antahonist".
- **Pixel Worlds by SdavGha** ([sdavgha.itch.io](https://sdavgha.itch.io)): *97 Buildings & Ruins*, *112 Market & Town Props*, *221 Village & Forest Props* and *202 Dungeon, Castle & Mine Props*: town buildings, houses, ruins, walls, lamps and market clutter. **This art is AI-generated** (Krea 2 Turbo, then cut, gridded and pixelised by SdavGha; tagged AI-generated on itch), so copyright protection of the base images is uncertain. Free for commercial games, edits allowed, no attribution required; do not resell or redistribute the files (even edited), do not use them to train AI/ML models, do not claim they are hand-made.
- **Dark Fantasy Isometric Castle – Free Sampler by Kyndrael** ([kyndrael.itch.io](https://kyndrael.itch.io/dark-fantasy-isometric-castle-free-sampler)): a few castle pieces (AI-assisted art, directed and edited by Kyndrael). Commercial use and modification allowed inside games; no resale, redistribution or sublicensing (raw or modified); no NFTs; no AI training, fine-tuning or datasets. "Art by Kyndrael".
- Town name plates and HUD pills use **Dark Ages UI** parts (`da-plate-gold`, `da-plate-grey`, `da-plate-crest-top`, `da-gem-grey`; see above).
